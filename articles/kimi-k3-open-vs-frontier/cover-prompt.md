# Hero-image prompts — Issue 012 · Kimi K3 · Open vs Frontier

The shipped `cover.png` is a clean vector hero (open acid padlock spilling "weights", two dim locked frontier vaults beside it). If you'd rather a photoreal / painterly hero from Gemini or another image model, use the prompts below.

Square 1:1. Brand palette: deep forest green `#1B4332`, neon acid-yellow `#E8FF3A`, warm cream `#F2ECD9`, soft mint `#D8F3DC`, coral `#FF8C61`. Keep the upper third text-free for a headline overlay.

---

## PRIMARY PROMPT (open vs closed — the hero)

> A bold editorial conceptual cover illustration, perfect 1:1 square, for a serious tech article about an "open" AI model breaking the grip of expensive "closed" ones. Centre-left: a single large padlock rendered in glowing neon acid-yellow (#E8FF3A), caught in the instant of springing OPEN — its shackle lifted and rotated, brilliant filament-light and tiny luminous particles streaming upward out of it like a model's weights being set free. To the right, receding into shadow: two smaller padlocks in matte cream (#F2ECD9), firmly CLOSED and dim, representing the locked commercial frontier. Background: a deep forest-green (#1B4332) void with a faint darker-green perspective grid receding to the floor, institutional and controlled. A few thin coral-orange (#FF8C61) sparks flicker where the open lock's shackle breaks free. Dramatic single-source rim lighting, volumetric glow around the open lock, high contrast, cinematic, subtle film grain. Mood: liberating but consequential — power changing hands, not chaos. Generous negative space in the upper third for a headline. Modern editorial poster aesthetic, premium magazine quality, flat-meets-3D hybrid illustration. No words, no text, no letters, no logos, no readable UI. Square aspect ratio 1:1.

## ALT A — "the price cliff" (economics angle)

> A striking 1:1 square editorial illustration about a cheap AI model undercutting expensive rivals. Three glowing vertical bars or luminous stacks of coins of descending height against a deep forest-green (#1B4332) background: the shortest bar rendered in brilliant acid-yellow (#E8FF3A) and clearly labelled-free by its glow, the two taller bars in dim cream (#F2ECD9), cold and grey. The short acid bar radiates warm light; the tall cream bars are in shadow. Thin coral (#FF8C61) accent line marking the gulf between them. Minimal, high-contrast, cinematic, premium poster design, clean negative space at the top. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos. Square 1:1.

## ALT B — "the recipe handed over" (open-source angle)

> A minimal, powerful 1:1 square editorial cover. A glowing acid-yellow (#E8FF3A) cube or box of light, lid open, releasing an upward stream of fine luminous particles and thin filament lines — a digital "brain" being given away — dissolving softly into a deep forest-green (#1B4332) background. Two dim cream (#F2ECD9) closed vaults sit small and cold in the lower corners. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, volumetric glow, subtle grain, restrained serious mood. Generous dark negative space for a headline. Premium tech-magazine aesthetic. No text, no letters, no logos. Square aspect ratio 1:1.

## Tips

- **Add the headline after.** Generate clean, then overlay "The free one that scared the expensive ones." in Space Grotesk with an acid-yellow Fraunces-italic accent, upper-third negative space.
- **Palette lock:** if colours drift, append *"strictly limited colour palette: forest green, acid yellow, cream, coral only."*
- **Keep it apolitical:** avoid flags or national symbols — the story is open vs closed, not China vs USA.
