Good questions

Before you book.

Do I need to pay for ChatGPT or Claude?

No. We start on the free versions. I'll only ever suggest a paid plan once it's clearly saving you time and paying for itself.

What do I need to get started?

A computer or laptop and an internet connection — that's it. I send a simple setup guide beforehand, and if anything's fiddly we sort it together at the start of your free session.

I'm really not techy. Is this for me?

Especially for you. Every session is jargon-free and goes entirely at your pace. There are no silly questions.

Is my information safe?

Yes. I'll show you simple, sensible rules to keep personal and confidential information out of AI tools from day one.

Will I get anything to keep?

Always a plain-English written summary after every session, plus the prompts we set up. Paid sessions also include a free recording and transcript, with your consent.

Can we meet in person?

The free taster is online. After that, in-person is available for longer sessions and workshops in the local area — I'll quote any travel clearly upfront.
