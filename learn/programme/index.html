Why bother, and why now

The gap is compounding. Quietly.

AI is no longer a tech story. It is being wired into how work gets done, worldwide, in every sector from retail to law to catering. The tools are improving on a monthly cadence, and the people using them well are simply getting more done in the same hours.

Here is the uncomfortable bit. In The AI Competency Pyramid I mapped where 20.6 million UK workers actually sit: roughly 95% are at the bottom levels, and the single jump that costs nothing is one that 97% still have not made. The gap between "typed a question into ChatGPT once" and "AI is built into my week" is not talent. It is habits, and habits are teachable.

Courses do not close that gap, which is why most people have three unfinished ones. What closes it is working on your actual work with someone who does this every day, then measuring whether it stuck. That is the whole programme: small time, small money, real tasks, and a score that goes up or does not.

No jargon, no hype, no twelve-module commitment. British scepticism welcome.
