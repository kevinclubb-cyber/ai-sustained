About the author

Senior business analyst. AI super-adopter. Still learning.

I'm a senior business analyst with twenty years of delivery behind me, across retail, fashion, finance and now hospitality and education catering. The last eighteen months have been the unglamorous end of AI, done properly: a governed data platform and the BI that sits on it, a CRM rollout, semi-autonomous agents running real backlogs, and a library of reusable AI skills that turned days of compliance work into minutes. Real production systems, not demos.

AI Sustained is where I write it up. Practical, evidence-led, and aimed at people who want to use AI well, not people who want to be sold something. The editorial is the thinking; the case studies are the receipts.

If something here is useful, share it. If something is wrong, tell me. If you want to talk shop, find me on LinkedIn.
