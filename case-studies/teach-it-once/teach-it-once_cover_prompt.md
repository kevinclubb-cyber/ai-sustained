# Cover prompt — Case Study 004 · "Prompts forget. Skills remember."

**Target:** 1920×1080 PNG. Save as `cover.png` in `/case-studies/teach-it-once/`
(referenced by og:image / twitter:image). Downscale a 1200×675 copy for the
index card if needed.

**Concept:** the written procedure as the hero. Not a robot, not a glowing
brain — a worn, tabbed operations binder open on a tidy desk in a dark office,
one acid-lime page tab catching the only light. The binder is the skill: rules
written down once, permanent, findable. The pen is the scale cue and the
human sign-off.

## Prompt

```
A well-used ring binder of printed procedures standing open on a tidy desk
in a dark, empty office at night, its pages divided by index tabs, one
single tab and the edge of one page catching a narrow beam of light; a
capped fountain pen resting beside the binder, a closed laptop pushed to
the edge of the frame. Cinematic editorial photography, shallow depth of
field, high fidelity, physically plausible lighting, single dominant light
source from a desk lamp just out of frame, film grain, muted shadow
contrast, 16:9, negative space in the upper third.
Colour palette locked to deep forest green (#1B4332) and near-black
(#0A0F0C) as the dominant tones, warm cream (#F2ECD9) for the pages and
any light surfaces, and exactly one accent of electric acid-lime (#E8FF3A)
as the lit index tab and the light it catches. No teal, no cyan, no
magenta, no purple, no orange.
Composed and still.
```

## Negative prompt

```
No glowing blue brains, no circuit-board motifs, no humanoid robots, no
binary digit rain, no neural-network node diagrams, no floating holographic
UI, no lens flare, no teal-and-orange grade, no stock-photo handshake, no
legible text on the pages, no watermarks, no logos, no distorted hands or
faces, no crowded composition, no neon cyberpunk city.
```

## Alt text (use in any inline placement)

A worn ring binder of tabbed procedures open on a dark desk, one acid-green
index tab lit by a narrow lamp beam, a capped pen resting beside it.
