The AI Survival Score (learn hub)

If you take the AI Survival Score at ai-sustained.com/learn, your quiz answers are scored in your browser. When you enter your first name and email to see the breakdown, I store in HubSpot: your name, email, your overall score, your result tier and your weakest pillar. I use this to send you your pillar-by-pillar report and the weekly practical-fix emails from AI Sustained.

The legal basis is your consent, given when you submit the form. Every email has an unsubscribe link; unsubscribing stops the weekly fixes immediately. You can also email me to have your score data deleted entirely.

Booking and sessions

If you book a taster or paid session, the booking runs through HubSpot Meetings, which stores your name, email and the appointment details alongside your contact record. Session notes I keep are limited to what's needed to prepare for and follow up your sessions, and are never shared. Payment details are handled by the payment provider named at booking — I never see or store card numbers.

Advertising

The site currently carries no advertising. If that changes, any sponsored content will be clearly labelled, and no advertiser will be given your personal data or allowed to set tracking cookies here. The position on partnerships is set out on the advertising page.
